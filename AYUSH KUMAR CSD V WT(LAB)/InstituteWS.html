About the Institute

Galgotia College of Engineering and Technology (GCET) is a premier engineering institute offering undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various fields of engineering, technology, and management.

Departments

1. Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning)

This branch focuses on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Data Analytics, Computer Vision, and Intelligent Systems. CSD is a specialized branch under the Computer Science & Engineering department that combines Computer Science with UI/UX Design, Human-Computer Interaction, Graphic Design, Animation, Product Design, and Software Application Development.

1. Computer Science & Engineering

Offers B.Tech programs with focus on programming, AI, data science, software engineering, and cybersecurity.

2. Information Technology

Focuses on software development, web technologies, networking, cloud computing, and database systems.

3. Electronics & Communication Engineering

Covers digital electronics, communication systems, VLSI design, embedded systems, and IoT.

4. Mechanical Engineering

Includes manufacturing technology, thermal engineering, machine design, robotics, and automation.

5. Civil Engineering

Deals with structural engineering, transportation, environmental engineering, and construction management.
